# Hazard occurrence

Hazard-event metrics come from the GHSL-UCDB **Hazard / Risk** theme.

## What it measures

- **Wildfire burnt area** — annual burnt area within the urban centre *(ha/year)*, recent record
- **Heatwave events** — count of recorded heatwave events
- **Drought events** — count of recorded drought events

## Temporal coverage

Wildfire burnt area is a recent annual **series**; heatwave and drought are **snapshot** event counts over the record. Hazard records are sparse — absence of an event is not absence of risk.

## Limitations — modeled, not measured

Event counts are derived from hazard catalogues and climate-event detection; heatwave and drought definitions vary by source and carry the "modeled" qualifier. These are occurrence records, not predictive risk ratings.
