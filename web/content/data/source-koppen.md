# Köppen-Geiger climate classification

A city's climate type, today and projected, comes from the **Köppen-Geiger** classification, pre-extracted to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **Köppen climate type** — the present climate class and its projected end-century class under SSP5-8.5

## Temporal coverage

A **projection pair**: present-day class versus the 2071–2099 class. Shown as a now/future toggle, never interpolated.

## Limitations — modeled, not measured

The future class is a **scenario-based projection**, not a forecast. A class shift (e.g. temperate → arid) signals direction of change, not a dated prediction.
