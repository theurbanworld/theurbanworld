# EDGAR emissions

Per-capita CO₂, total CO₂, all-greenhouse-gas (CO₂-equivalent), and PM₂.₅ emissions come from **EDGAR v8.0**, pre-allocated to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **Per-capita CO₂** — annual fossil CO₂ per resident *(t CO₂/person)* — headline metric
- **CO₂ sector fingerprint** — shares from energy, transport, industry, and residential sectors
- **Total CO₂** and **all-GHG** — annual totals *(t CO₂ / t CO₂-eq per year)*
- **PM₂.₅ emissions** — annual fine-particulate emissions *(t/year)*

## Temporal coverage

Annual from 1975, through 2020 for per-capita CO₂ and through 2022 for sector and total series.

## Limitations — modeled, not measured

EDGAR allocates **national** emission totals to space using population and built-up surface as proxies. Per-capita CO₂ is therefore partly circular with the population layer the observatory already shows. We always present per-capita CO₂ alongside its **sector fingerprint**, the least population-circular signal, and label every figure *modeled, not measured*. Independent emissions estimates are never blended into a single number.
