# Global Wind Atlas

Wind resource comes from the **Global Wind Atlas**, pre-extracted to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **Wind speed @100 m** — long-term mean wind speed at 100 m above ground *(m/s)*

## Temporal coverage

A single multi-year **climatology**, shown as a snapshot with no implied trend.

## Limitations

A resource indicator from mesoscale modelling and reanalysis; it is not a turbine-sited yield estimate and does not account for local obstacles, turbulence, or grid connection.
