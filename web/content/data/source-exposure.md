# Flood, coastal & marine exposure

Water and flood exposure come from the GHSL-UCDB **Exposure** and **Water** themes, which combine the population grid with return-period hazard and coastal-elevation models.

## What it measures

- **Population in the 100-year flood zone** — share of residents within the modelled 100-year return-period flood extent — headline **flood** metric
- **Population ≤10 m coastal elevation** — share of residents in the low-elevation coastal zone
- **Population exposed to cyclone wind** — share of residents within tropical-cyclone wind return-period zones
- **Local sea-level-rise rate** — recent marine trend for coastal cities *(mm/year)*

## Temporal coverage

Flood, coastal, and cyclone shares are **time series** on the population spine (1975–2030). Sea-level-rise rate is a **snapshot** trend.

## Limitations — modeled, not measured

Exposure shares depend on **return-period hazard models** intersected with the gridded population; they describe the share of people in a modelled hazard zone, not a probability of harm. Marine metrics apply only to coastal cities and render "not available" inland. Confirm the return-period model and baseline period against the UCDB fact sheets before citing specifics.
