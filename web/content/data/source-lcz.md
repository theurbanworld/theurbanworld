# Local Climate Zones (LCZ)

Urban morphology — the mix of built and land-cover forms that shapes the urban heat island — comes from the global **Local Climate Zones** map, pre-extracted to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **LCZ composition** — the share of each of the 17 Local Climate Zone classes (compact/open high-, mid-, low-rise; large low-rise; sparsely built; heavy industry; and natural land-cover types)

## Temporal coverage

A single **snapshot** of urban form; shown without a trend.

## Limitations

LCZ is a **classification** derived from Earth-observation features; class boundaries are approximate and a city's composition can shift as it is rebuilt. It characterises form, not temperature directly.
