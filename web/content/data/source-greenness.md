# Greenness & canopy

Livability greenness metrics come from the GHSL-UCDB **Greenness** theme, derived from Landsat NDVI and canopy-height products.

## What it measures

- **High-greenness built-up share** — share of built-up area with high greenness *(1985–2025)*
- **Mean greenness in built-up** — average greenness index within built-up area
- **Population with green-space access** — share of residents with green-space access (SDG 11.7)
- **Mean tree-canopy height** — average canopy height in built-up area *(m)*

## Temporal coverage

Greenness and green-space access are **time series** (1985–2025). Canopy height is a **snapshot**.

## Limitations

Greenness is an NDVI-based vegetation index, not a measure of biodiversity or green-space quality. Canopy height is a modelled snapshot from Earth-observation data.
