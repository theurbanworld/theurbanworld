# Global Solar Atlas

Solar photovoltaic potential — the headline **solar** metric — comes from the **Global Solar Atlas 2.0** (ESMAP / Solargis), pre-extracted to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **Solar PV potential** — long-term average energy yield of an optimally configured PV system *(kWh per kWp installed, per year)*

## Temporal coverage

A single multi-year **climatology** (long-term average), not a time series. It is shown as a snapshot with no implied trend.

## Limitations

PV potential is a resource estimate from satellite-derived irradiance and meteorological reanalysis; it reflects the resource, not installed capacity or generation. Local siting, shading, and grid access are out of scope.
