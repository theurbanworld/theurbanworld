# Urban form & land use

Urban-form metrics come from the GHSL-UCDB **SDG** and **land use / land cover** themes.

## What it measures

- **Land-use efficiency (SDG 11.3.1)** — the ratio of land-consumption rate to population-growth rate; values above 1 indicate land consumed faster than population grows (sprawl)
- **Built-up land cover** — built-up area *(ha)*, 2015 and 2019

## Temporal coverage

Land-use efficiency is a **decadal series**. Land cover is a two-point series (2015, 2019) shown without implying a continuous trend.

## Limitations

SDG 11.3.1 here uses UCDB's precomputed land-consumption and population rates; the observatory does not re-derive it from its own built-up rasters in this version. Two-point land cover is not a continuous time series.
