# C3S climate indices

Heat metrics come from **Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S)** bioclimatic and CMIP6 extreme indices, pre-extracted to urban centres in the GHSL-UCDB.

## What it measures

- **Warm days (TX90p)** — days per year above the local 90th-percentile maximum temperature, observed and projected (SSP5-8.5) — headline **heat** metric
- **Days UTCI > 32 °C** — days per year of strong heat stress on the Universal Thermal Climate Index, 1970–2020
- **Annual mean temperature** — observed and projected (RCP8.5) annual mean *(°C)*

## Temporal coverage

UTCI is a **time series** (1970–2020). Warm days and mean temperature are **projection pairs** — a present value and a modelled end-century value.

## Limitations — modeled, not measured

Future warm-day and temperature values are **scenario-based projections**, not forecasts, and carry the "modeled" qualifier. UTCI is a derived thermal-comfort index combining temperature, humidity, wind, and radiation.
