# Raw Idea

## Feature Description

**Task 2: City Selection** — Click or tap a city on the map to select it; highlight selected city boundary; pan/zoom to city extent `S`

**Task 3: City Info Panel** — Display selected city's name, country, population, area, and density with clean typography and layout `S`

These are foundational features for the Urban World observatory that will allow users to interact with cities on the map and see detailed information about them.
